Work/Fernway

Booking that clinics actually finish.

Fernway runs online booking for 40 physiotherapy clinics. Half of the people who started a booking gave up and phoned instead. I led the redesign, from the first interviews to the React components.

Client
Fernway Health
Role
Design lead, front-end
Team
1 PM, 3 engineers and me
Timeline
14 weeks, 2025

The problem

The old booking form asked for twelve pieces of information across five steps before showing a single available time. On a phone, 54% of people who started it never finished. Most of them rang the clinic instead, at exactly the time receptionists were busiest.

Clinics had started telling patients to “just call us”, which defeated the point of having online booking at all.

  • Times appeared on step four, so people did all the work before finding out nothing suited them.
  • One validation error cleared the whole form.
  • Receptionists re-typed almost a third of online bookings into the clinic diary by hand.

What I did

The fix wasn’t a prettier form. It was changing the order: show times first, hold the slot, then ask for only what the clinic needs to see the patient.

Listen

Sat on the front desk at five clinics and listened to 60 booking calls. Every one started with “have you got anything on…”.

Cut

Took the form from 14 fields to 5. Insurance, referral and address moved to an optional pre-visit check-in.

Prototype

Tested three flows with 24 patients, aged 19 to 81. The slot-first version won on every task, including for the oldest group.

Ship

Built the slot picker as a React component with the engineers, launched it behind a flag at four clinics, then everywhere.

What changed

Measured over the first three months across all 40 clinics, against the same three months a year earlier.

+38%more bookings completed online
−52%fewer booking phone calls
72smedian time to book, down from 4m 40s
4.8average rating after booking, out of 5

What I’d do differently

I’d bring the clinic receptionists into testing sooner. They spotted two edge cases in a day (family bookings and patients who need a longer first appointment) that took us a sprint to retrofit.

And I’d write the component documentation as we went, not in the last week. Future me was not grateful.
