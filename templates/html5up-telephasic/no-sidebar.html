New patients welcome

We're a family practice with four dentists and three hygienists, and we've been looking after smiles on Oak Avenue since 2004. Our patients range from three to ninety-three.

If the dentist makes you nervous, tell us. We'll take things slowly, explain everything before we do it, and you can stop at any time by raising your hand.

Your first visit includes a full check-up, X-rays if needed, and a clean with one of our hygienists. It takes about an hour, and you'll leave with a clear plan and prices.

We accept most insurance plans and offer interest-free payment plans for larger treatments like implants and Invisalign.
