Free lesson · Week 1

Find your window

The single most useful ten minutes in the course. Try it with any object on your kitchen table tonight.

  1. Turn off every light in the room.Mixed light is the reason most phone photos look yellow or grey. Window only.
  2. Put the object side-on to the window.Light from the side shows shape and texture. Light from behind you flattens everything.
  3. Hold a white card on the shadow side.It bounces light back and softens the shadow. A sheet of printer paper works.
  4. Tap and hold the brightest part, then slide down.Lock exposure there and darken slightly. Whites stay white and nothing burns out.
