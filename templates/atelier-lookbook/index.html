The house

We buy the cloth first

Most of what we make starts as someone else's surplus — a mill overrun, a cancelled order, a roll that missed a season. We buy what exists, then design to the metreage we have. It is the opposite way round to how this normally works, and it is why the runs are forty and not four hundred.

Nothing is dyed. The colours are the fibre's own, which means two coats from the same run are close but not identical. We have stopped apologising for that.
