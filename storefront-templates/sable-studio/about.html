The studio

Six people, deliberately

We have been offered the money to grow to twenty twice. Both times we worked out that it would mean the people you meet in the pitch would not be the people doing the work, which is the thing we started the studio to avoid.

So: six people, three or four projects at a time, and a waiting list when we are full. We would rather tell you we cannot start until May than start badly in February.

How we charge

Fixed price per stage

Not day rates. Each stage is quoted as a fixed price against a written scope, and the price does not change unless the scope does — in which case we requote before doing the work, not after.

Discovery

£6,000–£12,000

One to two weeks. Ends with a written problem definition and a fixed quote for the rest. If you take that document to another studio, no hard feelings — it is yours.

Design and build

£40,000–£180,000

Quoted after discovery. Invoiced monthly against stages completed, not hours burned.

Support retainer

From £2,400/month

Optional, rolling, one month's notice. Most clients take it for six months and then stop, which is the outcome we are aiming for.

Work we turn down

Being straight about this saves everyone a call:

  • Projects with a fixed launch date already announced publicly before scoping.
  • "Just build what is in the Figma" — if we cannot question the design, we are the wrong studio.
  • Anything where the budget is under £25,000. We would do it badly at that number.
  • Gambling, high-cost credit, and anything whose business model depends on people not reading carefully.
