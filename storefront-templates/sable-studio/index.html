How we work

Five stages, no surprises

Every stage ends with something you can look at and a decision you can make. If the answer at any gate is "stop", we stop and you have paid only for what is finished.

Scoping call

Ninety minutes, free, and often it ends with us telling you the project is smaller than you think or that you do not need us at all.

90 minutes · no charge

Discovery

We talk to the people who will use the thing, read your support tickets, and come back with a written definition of the problem. Sometimes it is not the problem you briefed.

1–2 weeks · fixed price

Design

Interface design in weekly cycles, reviewed every Friday. You see work in progress, not a reveal at the end.

3–8 weeks · weekly reviews

Build

Shipped to a staging environment from week one, so there is never a big-bang integration. Tests and documentation are part of the work, not a phase at the end that gets cut.

6–16 weeks · staged from day one

Handover and support

Your team works alongside ours for the last two weeks. Then a support retainer if you want one, and no hard feelings if you do not.

2 weeks · retainer optional
