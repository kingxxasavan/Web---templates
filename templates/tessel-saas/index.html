    FAQ

    Questions, answered.

    Is it really free for five people?

    Yes. Teams of up to five get the rota, the app and clock-in free forever, with one location. No card needed to sign up.

    Does my team need to download an app?

    It helps, but it isn’t required. Everyone gets their shifts by text and email too, and can see the rota on any phone browser.

    Can Tessel work out holiday pay and overtime?

    It tracks holiday allowance and flags overtime as you build the rota. The payroll export includes both, ready for your payroll provider.

    What if we have more than one site?

    The Business plan covers unlimited locations, with staff who can work across sites and a combined labour report.

    Can I import my team from a spreadsheet?

    Paste names, roles and pay rates straight from a spreadsheet, or invite people by text and let them fill in their own details.
