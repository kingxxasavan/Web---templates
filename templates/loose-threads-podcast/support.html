Questions

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. Cancel from the link in any members’ email and you keep everything until the end of the period you’ve paid for.

How do I get the ad-free feed in my podcast app?

After joining you get a private feed link. Tap it on your phone and it opens in Apple Podcasts, Pocket Casts, Overcast and most other apps.

Can I give a membership as a gift?

You can. Choose yearly billing, tick “This is a gift” at checkout and we’ll email a card to the lucky person on the day you pick.

Do you offer a student or low-income rate?

Email us and we’ll set you up as a Listener for free, no questions asked. Members on the Patron tier help make that possible.
