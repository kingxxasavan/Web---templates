Made by independent makers

Paper & Pine began as a market stall and a love of notebooks that open flat. Today we work with thirty independent makers, and we still test every pen on every paper before it goes on the shelf.

Our dot-grid notebooks use 100gsm paper that takes fountain pen ink without ghosting. The covers are bookcloth over board, and the spines are sewn so the pages lie flat from the first day.

Planning is personal, so our planners are undated. Start in March, skip a week on holiday, and pick up again without tearing out a page. Each one comes with a ribbon and a pocket for receipts.
