FAQ

Before you book

Can I get a refund?

Up to 60 days before the event, in full. After that, you can transfer your ticket to someone else for free until the day before.

Will talks be recorded?

Every talk is recorded and published free a month after the event. Workshops aren’t recorded, so the room can speak freely.

Is there a code of conduct?

Yes, and we enforce it. Everyone on site, from speakers to sponsors, agrees to it, and our safety team is reachable throughout both days.

Do you offer free or discounted tickets?

We give 60 free tickets to people from groups under-represented in tech, and 50% off for students and non-profits. Apply from the tickets page.
