The collection

Nine things, made properly

Every piece is finished by hand, so no two are identical. Small variations in glaze and grain are the point.

Why it costs what it costs

Fifty pieces, not fifty thousand

A production run of fifty means every piece passes through the same two pairs of hands. It also means we can't compete with a factory on price, and we've stopped trying.

What you get instead: a glaze that doesn't craze after a year, a handle that was pulled rather than moulded, and a repair service if you chip it.

Repair, don't replace

Chipped a mug? Send it back within three years and we'll repair it or replace it, postage covered.

Nothing plastic

Paper tape, moulded pulp, recycled card. If it arrives with plastic in the box, tell us — we got it wrong.

Made where we live

Everything is made in the Leeds studio. The furthest a piece travels before it reaches you is the post office.

Twice a year, when a new run lands

No weekly discount emails. Just a note when there's something new, and first refusal on short runs.
