Travel & stay

Getting here

Hartwell House is in the village of Aston, twenty minutes west of Oxford. Here’s everything you need for the weekend.

By train

Trains from London Paddington to Oxford take an hour. We’ve booked a coach from Oxford station at 12.45pm, back at midnight.

Coach seats are free

By car

Leave the M40 at junction 9 and follow signs for Witney, then Aston. Park on the lawn and leave your car overnight if you like.

Free parking

By air

Heathrow is about an hour away by car or coach. Birmingham is an hour and twenty minutes.

Airport transfers on request
