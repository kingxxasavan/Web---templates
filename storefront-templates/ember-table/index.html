What we do

One oven, one dough, no shortcuts

The dough is mixed on Sunday for the week and ferments cold for forty-eight hours, which is why it tastes of something before anything goes on top of it. The oven takes four hours to come up to heat and we light it whether we are busy or not.

The menu is short because everything on it is made here. When the sausage runs out on a Monday — and it does — that is the honest version of the trade-off.
