FAQ

Good questions.

Do you charge a call-out fee?

No. You pay the fixed price for the job, or for repairs the first hour, which includes travel. We tell you the cost before we start.

Are you Gas Safe registered?

Yes, every engineer who works on gas appliances is Gas Safe registered. They’ll show you their card when they arrive.

Do you guarantee your work?

All labour is guaranteed for 12 months. New boilers come with a manufacturer’s warranty of up to 10 years when serviced every year.

Can I pay monthly for a new boiler?

Yes. You can spread the cost over 12 months interest-free, or up to five years at 9.9% APR, subject to status.

What if it’s an emergency at night?

Call 0114 496 0123. An engineer is on call 24 hours a day for leaks, no heating in winter and other emergencies.
