Our story

It started with a wrong train.

Ada was going to Bath. Tom was going to Bristol. They were both on the train to Cardiff, and by the time the guard had sorted them out they’d agreed to get a coffee.

Seven years, two flats and one very spoilt cat later, Tom proposed on the same platform, with a ring hidden in a paper cup. Ada said yes before he’d finished the question.

We can’t wait to celebrate with the people who’ve been with us all the way. Thank you for being one of them.

Good to know

Can I bring a plus-one?

If your invitation says “and guest”, absolutely. We’ve kept numbers small, so we can’t stretch beyond the names on your invitation.

Are children invited?

Yes, there’s a children’s menu, a quiet room with films from 8pm and a babysitter on hand. Just add them to your RSVP.

Is the venue accessible?

The ceremony, dinner and dancing are all step-free. Tell us about any needs in your RSVP and we’ll make sure you’re comfortable.

Can we take photos?

Please keep phones away during the ceremony so our photographer can capture it. After that, snap away and share with #AdaAndTom.
