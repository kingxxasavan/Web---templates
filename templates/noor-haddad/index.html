About

I started in print, moved to product, and never stopped caring about the details nobody notices until they’re wrong.

Today I lead design at Fernway, a booking platform for physiotherapy clinics, and take on one or two freelance projects a year. I like small teams, hard problems and shipping on Thursdays. Outside work I teach a beginners’ front-end class and swim in the Atlantic far too early in the year.
