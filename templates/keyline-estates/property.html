A timber-clad barn conversion set in just over an acre of wildflower meadow, at the end of a quiet lane on the edge of Little Haddon. Converted in 2019 by an award-winning local architect, it pairs the original oak frame with underfloor heating, triple glazing and an air-source heat pump.

The heart of the house is a double-height sitting room with a wood-burning stove and glazed barn doors onto a south-facing terrace. A kitchen and dining room runs the length of the east side, with a separate boot room and utility. Upstairs, the main bedroom has a vaulted ceiling and a bathroom with a freestanding bath under the eaves.
