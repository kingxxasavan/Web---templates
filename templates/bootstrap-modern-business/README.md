# Ledgerly — Accounting software

A complete company website for a SaaS business: home, about, pricing, FAQ, blog, case studies and contact, nine pages in all.

Built for: B2B SaaS companies that need a complete marketing site.

## Start

Open `index.html` in a browser, or serve the folder locally. There is nothing to install or build. To publish, upload the whole folder to any static host: Netlify, Vercel, Cloudflare Pages or GitHub Pages.

## Pages

- `index.html`: Home
- `about.html`: About. Mission, history and team
- `pricing.html`: Pricing. Three plans with a feature list
- `faq.html`: FAQ. Accordion questions by topic
- `blog-home.html`: Blog. Featured and recent articles
- `blog-post.html`: Article. Long-form post with comments
- `portfolio-overview.html`: Customers. Customer story grid
- `portfolio-item.html`: Customer story. A single case study
- `contact.html`: Contact. Form and support options

## Make it yours

1. **Brand colour and fonts:** edit the variables at the top of `foundry-theme.css`. The fonts load from Google Fonts in each page's `<head>`.
2. **Words:** the copy is written for accounting software for small businesses. Replace the business name (Ledgerly), contact details and prices with your own in each HTML file.
3. **Pictures:** the illustrations in `foundry-art/` were drawn for this edition. Swap in your own photos at similar sizes.
4. **Forms:** they validate in the browser but send nothing. Point them at Formspree, Netlify Forms or your own backend before launch.
5. **Links:** replace the placeholder social links and addresses before publishing.
