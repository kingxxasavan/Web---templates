The residences

Every residence has a private balcony facing the bay.

Harbor Point is a collection of 84 residences on the edge of the bay, designed by award-winning architects Lund Hara. Every home has floor-to-ceiling glass and a private balcony facing the water.

Residents share a rooftop garden with outdoor kitchens, a fitness studio, a library lounge and a 24-hour concierge. Secure parking with EV charging is available for every residence.
