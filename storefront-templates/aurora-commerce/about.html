Our story

Two people, one kiln, since 2016

Aurora started because a mug broke. Not dramatically — the handle simply came away in the washing-up, eleven months after it was bought, from a shop that had already stopped stocking it.

What we changed

We tested glazes for two years before selling anything, because the failure that started this was a glaze problem pretending to be a handle problem. The one we settled on is lead free, food safe, and does not craze.

Handles are pulled rather than moulded. It takes about four times as long and it is the single reason the mugs cost what they cost.

Materials

Three, and that is all

Clay

Stoneware from Stoke

Dark-flecked, fired to 1260°C, which is what makes it chip-resistant rather than merely hard.

Linen

Grown in Normandy

Washed four times before it is cut, so it arrives already soft and will not shrink on you.

Wood

Olive, from offcuts

Sourced from furniture waste in Andalusia. Nothing is felled for a chopping board.

Come and see

The studio is open on the first Saturday of each month, 10am to 4pm. No appointment, no obligation to buy anything.
