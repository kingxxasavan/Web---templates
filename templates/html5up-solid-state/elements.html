Every element here is styled once in the stylesheet, so a change applies on every page. Keep paragraphs short and lead with what matters most to your visitors. Links, bold text and italic text are styled to match the rest of the site. Forms are ready to connect to a service such as Formspree or Netlify Forms. Buttons come in several sizes and styles for different calls to action.

Images can sit left, right or full width, with or without a caption. Tables suit price lists, opening hours and side-by-side comparisons. A quote block is a good place for a review from a happy customer. Spacing between sections stays consistent at every screen size. On a phone, columns stack so everything stays easy to read.
