The black oven

Two days to make. Ninety seconds to bake.

Our dough is flour, water, salt and a little yeast, left to prove slowly for 48 hours so it’s light, chewy and easy to digest. The oven, built by hand from Neapolitan clay, runs on oak and reaches 450°C. That’s what gives the crust its leopard spots.
