What it is like

Not a bootcamp

Nobody shouts. Nobody is going to make you do burpees until you are sick to prove a point. Sessions are structured, progressive and written down, so you can see what you lifted six weeks ago and why this week is heavier.

About a third of the room has never touched a barbell before joining. The Strength Foundations class exists entirely for them, and people stay in it as long as they want to.
