Our promise

Our house blend is roasted twice a week in small batches, with a rotating single origin on filter and loose-leaf teas from a family garden in Darjeeling.

Breakfast all day

Sourdough toast, shakshuka, buttermilk pancakes and a soup that changes daily. Our bakers start at four, so the cardamom buns are still warm at seven.

Coffee with roots

Buy our house blend by the bag, ground to order, or pick up a gift box with a mug, a bag of beans and a jar of our granola.
