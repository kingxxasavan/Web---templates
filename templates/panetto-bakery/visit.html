      Do you have gluten-free bread?

      Not yet: our kitchen is full of flour, so we can’t promise anything is free of traces. Our celebration cakes can be made with a gluten-free sponge.

      Can I freeze your sourdough?

      Yes. Slice it first, freeze it in a bag and toast from frozen. It’s nearly as good as fresh.

      Do you deliver?

      Only to wholesale customers for now. Order ahead and collect from either bakery instead.
