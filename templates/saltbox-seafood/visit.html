Good to know

Questions we get asked

Do you cater for people who don’t eat fish?

Yes. There’s always a vegetarian main from the grill and several small plates, and we can cook a steak with a day’s notice.

Are children welcome?

Very. We have high chairs, a short children’s menu and paper for drawing on. Before 7pm is usually calmer.

Can I bring my dog?

Well-behaved dogs are welcome at the bar and on the terrace, with water bowls by the door.

Do you sell oysters to take away?

From the hatch on the harbour side, Friday to Sunday. Bring them back for us to shuck, or we’ll show you how.
