Sizing

6 to 20

Every piece is cut on a body at a 14 and graded both ways from there, which is unusual — most houses cut a 10 and scale. It means the larger sizes are proportioned rather than stretched.

Measurements for every piece are on its page, in centimetres, taken flat. If you are between sizes in a coat, size down: they are cut generously.

Care

Less than you think

Undyed wool and alpaca want airing, not washing. Hang them somewhere with moving air overnight and most things resolve themselves.

Raw silk creases and always will. Ironing on low from the reverse works; steaming works better. Linen we do not comment on, because you already know.

Anything that needs repairing, send it back. We mend our own things for free, for as long as the workshop exists.
