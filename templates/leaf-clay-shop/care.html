Plant care, made simple

Most plants are lost to love: too much water, too little light. Here’s what actually matters, and a real person to ask when something looks wrong.

Light

Bright but indirect suits most houseplants. Succulents and bonsai want direct sun. If a plant leans, it’s asking to move closer to the window.

Water

Check with a finger, not a calendar. Water thoroughly when the top few centimetres are dry, then let it drain. Soggy roots kill more plants than drought.

Repotting

Every one to two years in spring, one size up. Bonsai are repotted with root pruning; book a workshop and we’ll show you how.

Delivery & returns

Good to know

How are plants packed?

In recycled cardboard with paper, wool and a moss collar to hold the soil in. No plastic, and all of it can go in your recycling or compost.

What if my plant arrives damaged?

Send a photo within 7 days and we’ll replace it. Our 30-day guarantee covers any plant that struggles despite following its care card.

Do you deliver everywhere?

Across mainland UK, Tuesday to Friday. Highlands and islands take an extra day.

Can I visit the greenhouse?

Yes, on Saturdays from 10am to 4pm, and for workshops. Directions are in your order confirmation.
