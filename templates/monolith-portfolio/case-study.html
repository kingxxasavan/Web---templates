Sound of
the Hull

Client
Monocle, issue 184
Year
2026
Role
Photography, art direction
Format
35mm, Portra 400
Output
14-page feature, 6 prints

Five weeks on a boat that has been in the same family since 1961, photographing a job that will probably not exist in twenty years.

The commission was two weeks. It became five because the fishing was bad and the interesting part — the waiting, the repairing, the arguing about quota — only becomes visible once the crew stops performing for the camera. That took about nine days.

Everything was shot on 35mm, partly for the grain and mostly because a film camera has no screen to crowd around. Nobody asked to see the back of the camera once in five weeks, which changed how people behaved in front of it.

The final edit was fourteen pages. The hardest cut was a frame of the skipper asleep in the wheelhouse at 3am — technically the best picture of the trip, and the one he asked me not to run. It stayed out.
