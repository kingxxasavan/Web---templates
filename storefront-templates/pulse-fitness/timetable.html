Class types

What each one actually is

Strength

Barbell Club · Strength Foundations · Saturday Strength

Squat, press, deadlift, on a written twelve-week progression. Foundations is the beginner version and moves at the pace of whoever is newest in the room. Bring a notebook or use the app; the point is that the numbers go up over months, not sessions.

Condition

Engine Room · Express Conditioning · Friday Partner

Rowing, bike, sled, carries. Hard, structured intervals rather than random exhaustion. Express is thirty minutes at lunchtime and you will not need a shower afterwards, which is the entire design brief.

Mobility

Hips & Shoulders · Restorative

Loaded stretching and positional work, run by a physio. Not yoga, though it borrows from it. If your squat is stuck this is usually the answer.

Skills

Olympic Lifting · Gymnastic Skills

Snatch, clean and jerk, and bodyweight work on the rings and bar. Capped at eight and ten respectively because these need hands-on coaching. Expect to spend the first month with an empty bar and to be glad of it later.
