Good to know

Before you ask

Allergies

Tell us when you book and again when you sit down. The kitchen is small and flour is everywhere, so we cannot promise a coeliac-safe environment — we would rather say that than take the booking.

Children

Very welcome before 7pm. Half portions of anything, no separate menu.

Large groups

Up to eight online. Nine and over, call — we can move tables but we need a day's notice.

Service

No service charge is added. If you want to tip, it goes to the floor and the kitchen in equal shares.
