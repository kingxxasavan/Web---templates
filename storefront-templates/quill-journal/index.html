The cost of a decision you never revisit

Every codebase carries a handful of choices nobody has looked at since the week they were made. The database that was right for eight users. The queue that exists because of an outage in 2021. None of them are bugs, which is precisely why they survive — and why they end up costing more than the bugs did.
