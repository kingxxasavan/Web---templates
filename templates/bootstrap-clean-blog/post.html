How I paid off $38,000 of debt in three years

The spreadsheet, the setbacks, and the habit that finally made it stick.

A new post every other Monday.

None of this needs a finance degree. It needs a list, a little patience, and the willingness to look at the numbers on a bad day as well as a good one.

Three years ago I owed $38,000 across two credit cards, a car loan and a student loan. I didn't have a plan, just a vague sense of dread every time the statements arrived.

The first step wasn't cutting back. It was writing everything down: every debt, every rate, every regular payment, in one spreadsheet. Seeing it all in one place was terrifying, and then it was useful.

I paid off the smallest balance first, then rolled that payment into the next one. Each win made the next one easier. By year three I was paying more toward debt than I'd ever spent on eating out.
