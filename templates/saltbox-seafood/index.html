Our story

A fish shed, a grill and a very short walk from the sea.

Saltbox started as a shack selling oysters to people waiting for the tide. Ten years on we have forty-two seats, a bar that looks out over the beds, and the same rule: if it wasn’t landed this week, it isn’t on the menu.
