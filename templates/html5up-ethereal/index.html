Kiln

New work by twelve ceramic artists. Harbour Gallery, 14 March to 30 June. Free entry.

The exhibition

Kiln brings together twelve artists who are changing what clay can do, from vessels thin enough to glow to sculptures fired in wood kilns for a week. Most of the work has never been shown before.

Twelve artists

Harbour Gallery is a ten-minute walk from Central Station. The exhibition is fully accessible, with step-free access, seating throughout and large-print guides at the door.
