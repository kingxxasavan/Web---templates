Case study

Northwind: one dashboard instead of nine

−41%support tickets
4 minto first report
11 wkdesign to rollout
340daily users

The problem as briefed

"Our dashboard is ugly and slow. Redesign it."

The problem we found

There was no dashboard. There were nine internal tools, built by four teams over six years, and the "dashboard" was the one people had a bookmark for. Redesigning it would have made the prettiest of nine tools nobody could reconcile.

What we did instead

Two weeks of discovery, sitting with twelve people across three departments. The output was a map of which tool owned which piece of truth — and three places where two tools disagreed about the same number, which nobody had noticed.

We built one interface over the existing systems rather than replacing them, with a permissions model that matched the org chart instead of fighting it. Rollout was department by department over eleven weeks, with the old tools left running until each group stopped opening them.

What we would do differently

We under-scoped the data reconciliation. Those three disagreeing numbers took three weeks to resolve, which we had not quoted for and absorbed. The lesson was to make "audit the data before quoting" part of discovery, which it now is.

"They spent the first fortnight telling us the thing we asked for would not work, and were right."

Priya Raghunathan — Operations Director, Northwind
