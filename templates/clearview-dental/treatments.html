Questions

Good to know

Do you see NHS patients?

We’re a private practice, which lets us offer longer appointments. Our membership plan keeps routine care affordable, and we’ll always tell you the cost before treatment.

I’m nervous about the dentist. Can you help?

Yes. Tell us when you book and we’ll allow extra time, agree a stop signal and talk you through everything. Sedation is available for longer treatments.

Do you treat children?

Children under 18 are seen free when a parent or guardian is a member.

What if I need to cancel?

Please give us 24 hours’ notice so we can offer the time to someone else. You can change your booking from the link in your confirmation email.
