Common questions

I have never lifted anything. Is this for me?

Yes, and roughly a third of the room was in exactly that position. Start with Strength Foundations.

Do I have to book?

For classes, yes — they cap at fourteen and the evening ones fill. Open gym never needs booking.

Can I pause my membership?

Up to eight weeks a year, for any reason, no questions and no fee.

Is there parking?

Free on Rutland Road after 6pm and all weekend. Otherwise it is a seven-minute walk from Shalesmoor tram stop.
