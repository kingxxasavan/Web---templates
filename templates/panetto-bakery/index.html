How we bake

Slow dough, short ingredient lists.

We bake the way bread used to be made: wild yeast, organic flour from a mill in Wiltshire, water, salt and a lot of time. No improvers, no shortcuts, nothing you can’t pronounce.

  • Organic flour, milled thirty miles away
  • Thirty-six hours of fermentation for every sourdough
  • Unsold bread goes to the Bristol food bank at close
  • Compostable bags, and 20p off if you bring your own
