Billing questions

How do you count people?

You pay for everyone who has at least one shift in a calendar month. Former staff and people without shifts are free.

Can I change plans later?

Any time. Upgrades take effect straight away; downgrades at the end of your billing period.

What happens after the 30-day trial?

You choose a plan, or drop back to Free if you have five people or fewer. We never charge without asking.

Do you offer discounts for charities?

Yes, registered charities and social enterprises get 30% off any paid plan. Just ask.
