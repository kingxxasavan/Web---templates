Works with your stack

Define checks in YAML or Terraform and keep them in the repo next to your code. Orbitly plugs into GitHub Actions, PagerDuty, Slack, Datadog and OpenTelemetry.

Orbitly sends real requests to your APIs from 18 regions every minute, checks the status code, the body and the latency, and tells you the instant something drifts out of bounds.
