Notes on the collection

Six pieces, four fabrics

The Long Coat

Cut from a mill overrun of undyed Yorkshire wool. Unlined, so it hangs rather than holds its shape, and heavy enough to stay closed without a belt. Forty made: twenty-two undyed, eighteen charcoal.

Column Dress

Raw silk from a cancelled order in Como. It creases, which is the point and also the most common thing people email us about.

Workshop Suit

Irish linen, sold as separates because nobody is the same size top and bottom and pretending otherwise is how suits end up unworn.

Heavy Knit

Alpaca, knitted by two people in Guimarães who have been doing it for thirty years between them. Each takes a day and a half.
