v4.221 Sep 2026

Shift swaps with manager approval

Staff can now swap or give away shifts in the app. Tessel checks every swap against your rules before it reaches you, so you only approve the ones that work.

  • newSwap and give-away requests in the app
  • newOne-tap approval from the notification
  • improvedSwap checks for overtime, rest breaks and roles
  • fixedNotifications could arrive twice on some Android phones

v4.12 Sep 2026

Labour forecast from your till

Connect your till and Tessel predicts each day’s sales from the last 52 weeks, then shows wages as a share of those sales as you build the rota.

  • newSales forecast for every day of the week
  • newLabour percentage under the rota
  • improvedFaster loading of the costs report
  • fixedBank holidays were missing from forecasts
v4.05 Aug 2026

A faster rota editor

We rebuilt the rota from scratch. It opens twice as fast, and dragging a shift now shows exactly where it will land.

  • newDrag-and-drop preview while you move a shift
  • improvedRota loads 2× faster for teams over 50
  • improvedKeyboard shortcuts: arrows move a selected shift
  • fixedCopying a week could skip Sundays
v3.914 Jul 2026

Holiday allowances

Set each person’s yearly allowance and Tessel keeps count as holiday is booked and taken, including part-timers.

  • newAllowance tracking in days or hours
  • improvedTime-off requests show remaining allowance
  • fixedHalf-day requests rounded up

Clock-in geofencing

Choose how close to work someone needs to be to clock in. No more clocking in from the bus.

  • newGeofenced clock-in, 50m to 500m
  • improvedClearer timesheet differences
  • fixedBreaks over midnight counted twice
