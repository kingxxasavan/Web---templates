Winter in the Faroes

Oaxaca wakes up early. By seven the market is loud with women selling tamales from steaming baskets, and the smell of chocolate and chilli follows you down every street.

There's no reason to rush on the Kumano Kodō. The trail climbs through cedar forests to shrines older than most countries, and every evening ends in a hot spring.
