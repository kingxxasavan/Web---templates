About

Up early, home late, usually wet.

I’ve photographed the mountains and coast of the north for fifteen years, mostly on foot, often alone and nearly always in the wrong weather. My work has been published by outdoor magazines and conservation charities, and hangs in homes in twelve countries.

I shoot on a medium-format camera and a heavy tripod, and I don’t add skies, composite images or remove things that were there. What you see is what was in front of me, on a good morning.
